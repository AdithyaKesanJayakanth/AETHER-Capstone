# Project logbook

Dated record of decisions, failures and fixes. Numbers quoted here are the ones in the case directories and `results/results.csv`; where an entry refers to an exploratory run that is not part of the final case set, it says so.

---

## June 2026 — geometry, conditions, meshing

**28 May.** Benchmark selection. Criteria: Mach ≥ 5, blunt body, laminar, published surface pressure *and* heating, perfect-gas-compatible regime. Chosen: (A) NASA TM 100484 (Wieting 1987), Run 31 — undisturbed Mach 8.03 flow over a 3-inch cylinder in the Calspan 48-inch shock tunnel, tabulated p/P₀ and q/Q₀ (planar, solver heating check); (B) NASA TM X-1243 (Bertin 1966), clean Apollo command module at α = 0, M = 10.18, Re_D = 1.1 × 10⁶ in AEDC Tunnel C — pressure and heating distributions over the heat shield and the model geometry (Fig. 1b). Rejected: Apollo flight data (reacting flow), Mach 17 shock-tunnel cylinder data (high-temperature effects), software tutorials (not independent evidence).

**29 May.** Conditions. The main condition is Benchmark B at model scale (D = 6.92 in = 0.17577 m). The report gives M and Re_D but not the run's stagnation temperature; I take T₀ = 1900 °R (1055.6 K), inside the Tunnel C envelope (1650–1950 °R), and a 300 K cold wall. Derived free stream: T∞ = 48.6 K, p∞ = 190.6 Pa, U∞ = 1422 m/s, p₀ = 9.1 MPa (1320 psia, inside the tunnel's 200–1900 psia range). Run 31 of Benchmark A has the complete state in Table 11 (p∞ = 0.124 psia, T∞ = 224.9 °R, T_T = 2849 °R, wall corrected to 530 °R). Written up in docs/conditions.md with the gas-model limits (Sutherland extrapolated to 49 K; vibrational excitation at T₀ ignored).

**1 June.** Geometry. Three design variables (R_n/D, R_s/D, θ_c), fixed D, r_b = 0.20 D, flat base. Construction with tangent joins written in `aether/geometry.py`; the Apollo 0.045-scale values reproduce the report's landmarks (cap/toroid tangency at S/R = 0.964 vs 0.965 stated; maximum radius at 1.082 vs 1.08). Validity rules and tests added. The labelled sketch is generated from the same code (docs/figure_geometry_baseline.svg).

**3–5 June.** Reference data. Run 31 tables transcribed from the report's Appendix E; Fig. 3a and Fig. 8a of TM X-1243 digitised from a 220 dpi scan (uncertainties stated in the file headers). Noted that the report's heating is normalised by the measured stagnation value and that Fay–Riddell (perfect gas) gives 487 kW/m² for Run 31 against the report's Q₀ = 721 kW/m²: the absolute level and the shape must be compared separately.

**8–12 June.** Mesh generator. Single-block structured grid built from the parameter table: wall-orthogonal boundary-layer cells, normals smoothed outward, far boundary as a smooth "egg" (circle + ellipse) intersected by the grid lines, far-field spacing relaxed toward uniform. First version projected the wall clustering into the far field as dense bands and had a lumpy outer boundary; fixed by the ray/egg intersection and the level redistribution. Three nested levels (ratio 1.5). Quality report (signed areas, angles, aspect ratio) written per case.

**15 June.** Gmsh evaluated against the structured generator; decision and reasons in docs/mesh.md (wall-orthogonal nested refinement for heat flux; exact refinement ratio for Richardson extrapolation).

**23 June.** SU2 v8.5.0 released on 22 June; pinned. Built natively (arm64, Open MPI 5.0.11); the official macOS binary (x86_64, Rosetta, serial) kept only for smoke tests. Both read the meshes and run. First full runs: with the adaptive CFL controller started at 0.5 and capped at 5, the controller collapsed to its floor whenever the residual oscillated, and 3000 first-order iterations left the residual at 10⁻³ with drifting drag. Fixed CFL 3 blew up at ~700 iterations. Lesson: start-up transients at Mach 10 on a 3 µm wall cell are the issue, not the scheme.

**24–25 June.** Numerical-scheme tests on the coarse cylinder (exploratory, scratch runs; summarised in docs/validation.md A.2). AUSM+-up2, AUSM, HLLC-type and Roe (entropy fix 0.1) all stalled with a kinked bow shock at 40–50° — the carbuncle instability on this shock-aligned grid. Roe with entropy fix 0.3 converged to 10⁻⁶ in 500 iterations but tripled the wall heat flux (the fix dissipates the boundary layer too). SLAU2 with the exact flux Jacobian converged with the heat flux at the experimental level (0.72 MW/m² on the coarse mesh against Q₀ = 0.72 MW/m² from the history monitor; see 30 June for the correction of what that monitor measures). JST aborted on a configuration conflict (gradient-reconstruction option); AUSM+M aborted. Decision: SLAU2, first-order start-up stage with CFL 1 → 50, second-order restart.

## July 2026 — solver investigation, validation, study

**26 June.** Capsule runs. First-order stage stationary; second-order stage oscillated: drag ±20 %, stagnation heating by a factor 2–5, with the adaptive CFL flipping between 1 and 50. Field inspection: pressure above the pitot value on the nose, Mach-26 cells and 0.5 K temperatures at the sharp cone/base corner (expansion to near vacuum).

**27 June.** Fillet. The sharp corner replaced by a fixed r_f = 0.03 D fillet in the closure definition (docs/geometry.md, "Why the fillet"); minimum mesh angle 17° → 39°. Second-order still non-stationary.

**29 June.** Wake exclusion. Forebody + cone domain with the outlet normal to the cone end; base-drag bound ΔC_D ≤ 0.0006 derived and documented. The laminar near wake was feeding a limit cycle into the whole field; without it the first-order stage converges to 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁷. Second-order still non-stationary on the capsule face, stable on the planar cylinder.

**30 June.** Two findings while tracing the second-order noise with solution snapshots. (i) SU2's history field MAXIMUM_HEATFLUX is not the largest wall-node heat flux (serial check: 723 kW/m² reported against 546 kW/m² at the hottest node), so peak heating is now evaluated from the wall nodes of the volume solution; the history field is used only as a stationarity monitor. (ii) The axisymmetric sphere test — the validated cylinder mesh run with AXISYMMETRIC = YES — shows a heating dip to 154 kW/m² at the second wall node between 504 at the axis and a 640–705 plateau (Fay–Riddell sphere value 652), with the pressure and temperature profiles of the first three columns next to the axis visibly wrong. Not cured by moving the symmetry line off the axis (10⁻⁶–10⁻⁴ m). SU2 issues #1373 (2021) and #2438 (open, 2025) describe the same defect; SU2 v7.5.1 built and tested: worse (70 kW/m² at the axis node).

**1 July.** Patch. Pull request #2715 to SU2 ("Axisymmetric near-axis fixes using L'Hôpital-consistent formulations", open since 22 January 2026) changes two source files; applied to the v8.5.0 tree (`tools/pr2715.diff`), rebuilt. Sphere test now monotone and smooth (848, 829, 794, 770 … kW/m²), pressure smooth, residual to 10⁻⁹·⁶ in 850 iterations. Hemispherical capsule nose: smooth profile. Apollo baseline first-order: stagnation pressure 1 % below pitot, heating flat across the face (99 → 91 kW/m²), shoulder peak at S/R = 0.96, drop to 16 kW/m² at S/R = 1.08 — the shape of Bertin's Fig. 8a. This binary is the project solver; the decision and evidence are in docs/validation.md A.3.

**2 July.** Second order in axisymmetric mode. With the patched solver, MUSCL + limiter on the capsule remained non-stationary for every combination tried: SLAU2 / AUSM+-up2 / AUSM / HLLC, Venkatakrishnan / Venkatakrishnan–Wang (0.05, 0.3) / Barth–Jespersen / van Albada, fixed CFL 1–3 or adaptive, limiter frozen, weighted-least-squares reconstruction, Euler-wall axis. Symptom in every case: stagnation pressure pulsating between 23 and 33 kPa (pitot 25.5), stagnation heating 220–520 kW/m². The identical configuration in planar mode is stationary (26.0 kPa, 95–106 kW/m²), and first-order axisymmetric is stationary and converged. Decision recorded in docs/validation.md A.4: axisymmetric production runs use first-order SLAU2; the order effect is quantified on the cylinder (both orders stable) and the discretisation error by the three-level mesh study with Richardson extrapolation. This is a limitation of the study and is stated as such.

**3 July.** Production started: cylinder benchmark (coarse/medium/fine, both orders) and baseline capsule (coarse/medium/fine). Results appended below as they complete.

**4 July.** Mesh refinement. With the patched solver and first-order SLAU2, refining every spacing by 1.5 (medium level) re-triggered the stagnation-line anomaly: planar cylinder 932 kW/m² at the stagnation node against a 460 kW/m² plateau; axisymmetric capsule stagnation pressure 29–32 kPa (pitot 25.5) with a heating spike. Tried and rejected on the medium cylinder: AUSM+-up2 with its pressure-diffusion coefficient raised (1.0 and 3.0 diverge; a two-line source change to make the coefficient overridable, kept in `tools/aether_su2_patches.diff` for the record), small Roe entropy fix (unphysical), NEMO with frozen air and AUSM+M (far from converged after 2000 iterations at CFL 0.5), a 3-D thin-wedge mesh with symmetry planes instead of the axisymmetric terms (stagnation pressure 1.67 × pitot). Separating the directions: refining the boundary layer alone (first cell 1.5 → 1.0 → 0.67 µm) keeps the cylinder solution smooth and changes the stagnation heating by less than 3 %; refining the tangential or shock-normal spacing produces the spike.

**5 July.** Mesh family redefined: the three levels refine the wall-normal boundary-layer resolution at fixed tangential and shock-region spacing (docs/mesh.md); the effect of the shock-region spacing is assessed separately (`cases/domain_check/base_shock16`). On the capsule the medium level shows a residual near-axis bump (+40 % within s/R < 0.05) while plateau and shoulder peak move by 1–2 %; the fine level with the gentle start converges. Start-up CFL changed to 0.5 → 30 (factor 1.2) after one divergence of the medium capsule at the old 1 → 50 setting.

**6 July.** Production restarted with the final settings: cylinder benchmark coarse/medium/fine (both orders), baseline capsule coarse/medium/fine, far-boundary and shock-spacing checks, then the design sweep at the base level.

**7 July.** The boundary-layer-only family failed its first production run: the base-level capsule, clean twice before, locked into the carbuncle branch after nothing but a change of the far-field stretching (16 → 24 outer cells). Two steady branches, then. A pressure-switched entropy fix for the Roe flux (two short source changes) was written to test whether the fix could be confined to the shock; it showed instead that the clean solution is a stable steady state of the Roe operator even with the fix removed, and that Roe's boundary-layer heat flux is ×3 for a different reason (wall-node temperature 20–50 K above the imposed value: dissipation of the entropy wave across the boundary-layer density gradient). Continuation test: Roe-fix start-up, SLAU2 restart — clean on the fully refined cylinder and capsule. Pipeline rewritten as three stages (docs/validation.md A.2).

**8 July.** Refinement family redefined as full refinement (every spacing ÷ 1.5 per level) starting at 0.8 mm shock spacing; the 1.2 mm level is dropped because it still locks into the carbuncle branch with the new procedure. Production restarted: cylinder coarse/medium/fine (three stages), baseline coarse/medium then fine, far-boundary check, sweep at the coarse level.

**9 July.** Second production attempt: the coarse baseline landed on the carbuncle branch in the SLAU2 stage despite a clean Roe start (stagnation pressure 8 % above pitot), while the same procedure on a scratch mesh with finer far-field stretching had stayed clean; from the same Roe state, SLAU2 at fixed CFL 1 stays clean, adaptive ramps jump branches, and the other first-order fluxes (AUSM+-up, SLAU, HLLC, CUSP) diverge or jump. Second-order Roe diverges in axisymmetric mode like SLAU2. Added a physical-branch check to the post-processor (stagnation pressure against the pitot value, stagnation heating against the shield plateau) and automatic stage-2 retries with gentler schedules; testing whether the far-field stretching is the trigger.

**10–11 July.** The far-field stretching turned out to be the difference between the scratch mesh that converged cleanly and the production mesh that did not (36 vs 24 stretched outer cells): with 36 cells and the start-up CFL cap of 30 the coarse baseline converged to the physical branch (stagnation pressure 0.1 % from the pitot value, plateau 90.5 kW/m², shoulder peak 116, drag flat to 0.02 %), with the two or three wall nodes next to the axis still oscillating. Decisions recorded in docs/validation.md A.5: heating objective evaluated outside S/R < 0.1, the stagnation-region heating represented by the plateau median, physical-branch check on the stagnation pressure with automatic stage-2 retries, SU2's global heat-flux monitor dropped from the convergence rule because it includes the axis cells. Cylinder benchmark completed on three levels (docs/validation.md A.1). Production relaunched for the capsule: baseline coarse/medium/fine, far-boundary check, sweep.

**12 July.** Baseline medium converged on the first attempt with no near-axis anomaly at all (axis cells within 3 % of the plateau), the coarse level still carries the oscillation out to S/R ≈ 0.2: the sweep is therefore run at the medium level (49 000 → 110 000 cells, about 30 minutes per case on four ranks) and the coarse level serves only the mesh study. Far-boundary check at the medium level: drag changes by 0.01 %, peak heating by 0.05 %. The fine baseline and the sharp-shoulder case S03 both landed on the carbuncle branch in their first SLAU2 attempt and are being retried automatically; N08 converged first time. Sweep running as two streams.
